# Community Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

We want this project to remain friendly, professional, evidence-driven, and open to
participants of different experience levels, languages, identities, and abilities.

In discussions, issues, pull requests, and reviews:

- Discuss facts, reproduction steps, and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, copyright, image rights, and pattern-license boundaries.
- Accept that domain expertise and physical test crochet may correct software assumptions.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, expose personal information, or publish material
  unsuitable for professional collaboration.
- When a risk is found, help limit its impact and disclose vulnerabilities privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this policy and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation, explaining the reason where practical. For a private
community report, contact the maintainer through a private method on the repository
owner's GitHub profile. Use [SECURITY_EN.md](SECURITY_EN.md) for vulnerabilities.
